Context

Designed an immersive digital platform and membership experience built to significantly elevate user engagement and drive sustained club acquisition.

Role

Product Designer

Timeline

3 Weeks

Core features

  • Seamless Navigation: Architected a high-contrast, intuitive interface to ensure effortless usability for users of all technical skill levels.

  • Therapeutic Personalization: Implemented customizable wellness paths that allow members to blend fitness routines with mental therapy sessions.

  • Performance Optimization: Optimized for accessibility and speed, ensuring a premium, frictionless journey on both mobile and desktop.

Statistics and Secondary Research

Initial discovery began by auditing existing platform metrics alongside qualitative user research. Analyzing this combination of quantitative drop-off data and remote user feedback exposed severe friction in the user journey. Specifically, it highlighted a stark mismatch between the platform's high-pressure visual aesthetic and the actual daily habits of a multi-generational membership base.

Findings from Primary Research

Synthesizing secondary research findings alongside targeted usability studies and depth interviews enabled a comprehensive analysis of a diverse cohort of members—ranging from young fitness enthusiasts to elderly wellness seekers. The primary objective was to uncover unique barriers faced by each demographic when engaging with the digital platform.

Light Mode

Optimized for a stress-free interface; designed to reduce cognitive load during therapy sessions.

Quick Access

High-visibility navigation ensuring essential resources remain within a single interaction.

Quick Response

Adopted a mobile-first architectural approach to guarantee high-performance across all device breakpoints.

The Redesign

After a thorough analysis of platform performance and user requirements, the design direction was established to bridge the gap between high-pressure fitness aesthetics and accessible, therapeutic wellness.

The Hero

Designed to command immediate attention, the hero section functions as a high-impact entry point. Prioritizing strategic visual hierarchy and interactive elements establishes instant brand authority and guides user focus toward core value propositions.

Strategic Conversion

The initial layout suffered from fragmented user pathways and unclear conversion signals. This was resolved by implementing a cohesive, data-driven CTA strategy, utilizing concise, benefit-oriented copy paired with high-contrast visual cues to streamline the decision-making process.

Dynamic Engagement

Primary research identified a critical demand for high-fidelity engagement. To meet this expectation, the platform was architected with intuitive, responsive interaction patterns, ensuring that every touchpoint serves as a functional step toward the final user goal.
